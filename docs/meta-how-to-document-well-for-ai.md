- We document mainly for AI, not humans. Teammates tend to not read docs, and they're better off asking AI about the codebase just-in-time anyway instead of reading a mountain of docs.
- Most of what you manually document is probably redundant vs what the AI quickly figures out. Instead of writing out potentially overlapping knowledge, ask AI e.g. "check this codebase's routing system and ask me nuanced questions about what you don't know. Let's do a few rounds of Q&A". Afterward, ask AI to make a summary of the interview. This way you only write down tokens for what AI truly doesn't know. Much quicker feedback loop and concise docs for AI. This is akin to plan mode but, until further development, simpler/more customizable.
- When you're one-shotting a feature through the spec, make sure to ask AI to modify the spec based on its iteration. Otherwise the spec goes stale.
- It's hard & time-wasting to build evals to see if AI is getting more out of your docs. Instead, a trick is to just read the AI's chain of thoughts when you modify your docs, and see if it's picking up what you want it to.
